# Template: the self scan, T+15 min

Posted fifteen minutes after launch, in the room and on X. VITALS scans its
own launch with the same command anyone else gets, and posts the card without
editing it.

Fifteen minutes because that is where the card has something to say: the
opening tax window closed at three seconds, the first buyers are in, and the
thirty minute benchmark rung is not yet due. Earlier than that and half the
card reads "undetermined", which is honest and not worth posting.

## What to run

```
/full <CA>          in DM, to read it first
/image <CA>         the card as a picture, for X
```

The card is not rewritten for the post. If a line says undetermined, that
line goes out saying undetermined.

## The post

```
VITALS, fifteen minutes in. Its own scan, same card as anyone else gets.

<paste the card exactly as the bot rendered it>

The opening window: <N> wallets tax exempt at launch, which is the deployer
and nobody else. Dev buy <X>% of supply. Creator tax <Y> bps.

Seat numbers are public, names are not. The first ledger post is at T+4h.

Read it yourself: t.me/vitalscheck_bot
```

## Rules for this post

- The card goes out as rendered. No line is dropped because it is unflattering
  and none is added because it is flattering.
- No price, no direction, no "we are up", no chart.
- No exclamation marks.
- If the scan comes back with something undetermined, say which and why. A
  self scan that only prints the parts that worked is an advert.
- If the card shows a finding on our own launch, the finding goes in the post.
  A scanner that hides its own 🚩 is worth nothing.
- No claim about anyone we have not launched with. No partner names.
